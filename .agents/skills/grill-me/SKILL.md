# Grill Me — Design Discovery

Use for substantial design and website requests where references, goals, audience, brand character, interactions or assets are under-specified.

## Protocol
1. Inspect the current repository and user-provided references first.
2. Choose the single unknown whose answer would most change the design direction.
3. Ask one conversational question, not a form and not a list of questions.
4. Prefer concrete examples or a vivid scenario when they reduce ambiguity.
5. After the answer, reflect what it means for the design and ask the next most useful question.
6. Stop interviewing when two consecutive answers add no useful information; summarize the choices and produce a creative brief.
7. Before large implementation work, present the brief and get approval. Do not repeat questions already answered.
8. Record confirmed decisions in a project brief; keep assumptions labelled.

## Suggested sequence (adapt to answers)
- Desired emotional reaction on the first screen.
- Art direction and references.
- Main CTA and conversion goal.
- Catalog interaction choreography.
- Product detail, close and return behavior.
- Founder photography and product asset availability.
- Mobile motion compromise and reduced-motion fallback.
- Stack, deployment and definition of done.

## Output
An approved brief with audience, promise, art direction, color, typography, sections, assets, animation storyboard, mobile behavior, accessibility, performance budget and acceptance tests.
